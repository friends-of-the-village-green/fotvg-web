# Who FotVG is

Curated from FotVG's own documents (Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, the mission and
vision statement, and board minutes from September 2025 to June 2026). This is the
reference for anyone writing copy for the site.

> **Read this before writing any user-facing text.** The single easiest mistake on this
> project is conflating the three organizations that share the "Village Green" name.

---

## The three Village Green organizations

Three separate legal entities. Visitors — and journalists, and grant reviewers — mix
them up constantly. From the September 2025 inaugural board meeting:

| Entity | What it is | What it owns / does |
|---|---|---|
| **Village Green Metropolitan Park District** (MPD, VGMPD) | A municipal park district | Owns **the land**. Votes on budget, *not* programming. |
| **Village Green Foundation** (VGF) | Nonprofit | Owns **the building**. Legacy giving. |
| **Friends of the Village Green** (FotVG) | 501(c)(3), founded 2025 | **Programs and people.** |

FotVG's own shorthand for the split is exactly that: *"FotVG – Programs and people."*

**Implications for the site:**

- Never imply FotVG owns, runs, books, or maintains the building.
- The Village Green Community Center is the *place*. FotVG is one of several
  organizations that make things happen there.
- The board's marketing advisor noted that *"people don't think of the Village Green as
  a municipal park district."* Don't try to fix that on FotVG's site — just be accurate
  about FotVG's own role.
- A memorandum of understanding between FotVG and the MPD was still in progress as of
  May 2026. Don't describe the relationship as more formal than it is.

Other organizations housed in or partnered with the Village Green Community Center:
**Boys & Girls Club**, **Friends of the Kitsap Regional Library** (KRL), **ShareNet**
(food bank), **Kingston Cares**, **North Kitsap Fire Department**, and the **Ladies
Musical Club of Seattle**.

## Legal identity

- **Legal name:** Friends of the Village Green (Articles of Incorporation render it
  "The FRIENDS OF THE VILLAGE GREEN")
- **Abbreviation:** **FotVG** — their own consistent usage. Not "FOTVG".
- Washington nonprofit corporation, incorporated **14 July 2025**
- Bylaws adopted **19 August 2025**
- **501(c)(3)** determination received 2025; donations are tax-deductible
- Registered with the Washington Secretary of State

**The corporation has no members.** Bylaws Article 2 and Articles of Incorporation
Article VI both say so; the board may add a membership option later. Until then,
"become a member" is factually wrong on this site. Calls to action are **Donate**,
**Volunteer**, and **Come along**.

Not for publication: the EIN, the registered agent's home address, bank and Square
account details, and insurance policy numbers. All of these appear in the source
documents.

## Mission, vision, values

**Mission** (as adopted, and as quoted in FotVG's own donor letter):

> Connect and empower community members to foster the long-term vitality of the Village
> Green Community Center, through development and support of programs and services that
> meet the needs and interests of our changing and expanding community, making the
> Village Green a welcoming hub for growth and the opportunity for all to participate.

**Vision:**

> The Village Green offers and supports activities and services that enrich lives, build
> relationships and inspire all to thrive every day.

**Values:**

- Openness to community ideas
- Interest in learning
- Welcoming all ages and abilities
- Inclusive free community programs

people of all ages and abilities."* It reads well and is consistent with the vision
statement, but no board adoption of it appears in the minutes — earlier candidates

## What FotVG actually does

### The four pillars are dead — settled 9 August 2026

Earlier source documents described FotVG's work as four pillars, in two versions that
never got reconciled (September 2025 minutes: Arts and Humanities / Social Services /
Health and Fitness / Science-Greenworks. Mission & structure document: Humanities (VGAP)
/ Community Services / Fitness / Science and Education / Social).

**That taxonomy is retired.** It does not structure the site, it is not the navigation,
and it should not appear in copy. The open question about which version was current is
closed — neither. The **three program areas** below are the organizing idea.

Left here only so that nobody rediscovers the pillars in the minutes and reintroduces
them.

### The three program areas

Confirmed by Betsy Cooper, 8 August 2026. **These three, and only these three, are what
FotVG currently supports.** Each gets its own page on the site, carrying its mission,
vision, and related information. Program attributions in event titles (for example
"Music at the Green · Village Green Arts Program") link to the relevant page.

- **Village Green Arts Program (VGAP)** — arts and humanities. The *Sunday Sounds*
  concert series, *Music at the Green* (summer, outdoors), and rotating exhibitions in
  the Grand Hallway Gallery. Partners with the Ladies Musical Club of Seattle. Chaired
  by the FotVG vice-president. Moved from MPD fiscal sponsorship to FotVG in December
  2025. *Cello Mania* is a VGAP event.
- **Greenworks** — community gardening, the natural sciences, and the natural world.
  See the mission and vision below. Transferred from the Village Green Foundation to
  FotVG at the end of 2025.
- **Community building** — community projects that raise money and build coalitions.
  The NU2u rummage sale and the Secret Garden Tour both sit here.

FotVG hopes to facilitate and support **more** activities and community engagement over
time, to meet the needs of a changing and growing Kingston. Say that; don't imply the
current three are the ceiling.

### Greenworks — mission and vision

Verbatim from *VILLAGE GREEN – GREENWORKS*, the Google Doc shared by Kathleen Bullivant
(Greenworks Coordinator) on 9 August 2026. Use this wording on the Greenworks page.

> **MISSION** is to offer and provide a conduit for connecting the local community to
> the four major divisions of horticulture; Floriculture, Landscape, Olericulture
> (vegetables) and Pomology (fruits).
>
> **VISION:** Using the Village Green, Kingston, as its hub Greenworks throughout the
> year will offer and partner with local groups to offer educational classes, hands on
> workshops, lectures and events to support the rich and diverse horticulture
> opportunities available in our area.

Calls to action on that page: get involved and volunteer for events, suggest a workshop
idea or lecture topic, donate, sponsor an event. Coordinator: **Kathleen Bullivant**,
`kwagreenworks@gmail.com`. Greenworks events are publicized through the Village Green
website and the monthly newsletter.

- **Secret Garden Tour** — six private Kingston gardens, one day, ticketed. Held 13 June

  tenth anniversary.

## Governance

- **Executive committee:** President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer.
- **Board:** 5–13 directors on staggered three-year terms, elected by the board itself.
- At least **80% of directors must live within the North Kitsap School District**.
- The Village Green Community Center manager sits **ex officio, without a vote**.

**No paid staff.** The board are all volunteers and FotVG employs nobody. Where the
mission statement says FotVG "staffs" programs, it means *finding the people to run
them*, not employing them. FotVG's money does sometimes pay an individual — a musician's
fee, an instructor — but anyone paid to deliver a program is **not** a FotVG board
member or volunteer. (Confirmed by the board, August 2026.)

This is safe to state publicly, and it is the strongest line FotVG has for a donor: what
you give reaches the program because there is no salary in front of it.

Board composition changes frequently. Do not hard-code names into templates — if board
members are listed on the site, they belong in the CMS, and only with each person's
agreement.

Board deliberations, resignations, staffing tensions, and funding disputes appear
throughout the minutes. **None of that goes on the website.**

## Voice

**American English throughout.** Spelling, vocabulary, and idiom. This is a Kingston,
Washington organization writing for its own town. See the hard rule in `CLAUDE.md`.

FotVG writes plainly and warmly. It is a group of neighbors helping keep a much-loved
building full of things to do — an **all-volunteer** group, with no paid staff, that
supports *some* of what happens at the Village Green rather than running the place.

**Say that FotVG is new.** It was founded and granted 501(c)(3) status in 2025 — it is
only a few months old. Betsy asked for this to be made plainly. It reframes a thin
events list and a short history as the natural state of a young organization finding its
feet, and it is an invitation to help build something rather than an apology.

The best available source of authentic voice is the anniversary post-it exercise, where
visitors answered "What you love about the Village Green" unprompted. The answers were
short and concrete: the garden, the people, the art, the friendship, the front desk
staff, Pie-in-the-Park, "very open and welcoming", "kindness", "staff, activities, for
everyone, all ages and free or affordable".

That is the register to aim for. `[TK: confirm with board that anonymous post-it
responses may be quoted publicly.]`

Recurring value words in FotVG's own documents: **welcoming**, **all ages and
abilities**, **free or low-cost**, **barrier-free**, **intergenerational**,
**inclusive**.

## Practical constraints on copy

**Search visibility.** Board minutes record that leading with "friends" or "village
green" produces too many wrong results on Google. Page titles and meta descriptions
should anchor on **Kingston, Washington** and **Village Green Community Center**. Never
ship a bare `<title>Friends of the Village Green</title>`.

**Contact address.** The current GoDaddy site publishes the registered agent's **home
address** as the organization's contact address. That should not carry over. Use the
Village Green Community Center's address. `[TK: confirm the Village Green Community
Center's street address.]`

**Logo.** Board decision, May 2026: *"FotVG in name only for now, no illustration."* A
rope-motif draft was rejected in February 2026. Green was discussed as a unifying
color, and an "open door" motif was floated but not adopted. Use a type-only wordmark;
do not generate or commission a pictorial mark.

**Donations.** Square is FotVG's existing card processor — they use it at events and own
card readers. The donate button links out to Square.
