There is nearly always something on.

We’re Friends of the Village Green — neighbours who fund and run programs at the Village Green Community Center in Kingston. Concerts, a garden, lunches, lectures, trail work. Most of it free or low-cost, all of it open to every age and ability.

What we’ve done

Every event gets written up afterwards, with photos. It’s how we show what your money paid for.

Summer 2026

Music at the Green

Free outdoor concerts in the Kingston Village Green Park, every Wednesday evening through July and August. People bring their own chairs, and a good deal of food. By the second set there is usually dancing.

Photos: Betsy Cooper

Sunday, February 22, 2026

Sunday Sounds: Cello Mania

Four cellists in the Summit room, playing folk, classical and contemporary pieces to a full house. Seating is limited for these, so they are worth booking early.

Photo: Betsy Cooper

November 2025

The Grand Hallway Gallery

The corridor doubles as a gallery, and the work on the walls changes through the year. It costs nothing to walk down it, which is rather the point.

Where the money goes

What we pay for

  • Village Green Arts Program (VGAP) Sunday Sounds concerts, Music at the Green through the summer, and the exhibitions in the Grand Hallway Gallery.
  • Greenworks The community garden and its rental beds, plus classes, workshops and lectures. The 2026 beds sold out.
  • Village Green Woods Three acres of woodland above the upper parking lot. Volunteers build and clear trails on Wednesday mornings.
  • Lunches, lectures and welcomes The monthly senior luncheon with Kingston Cares, the newcomers series with the library, and four lectures a year.

Money

Donate

A donation pays a musician, buys seed and tools, or covers a lunch for someone who needs one. We have no paid staff and the board are all volunteers, so what you give goes to the program.

Volunteer

Wednesday mornings in the woods. An hour on the front table at Discover Kingston. Setting out chairs before a concert, and stacking them after. Tell us what you’d enjoy and we’ll find you something.

Three organizations share the Village Green name. The Metropolitan Park District owns the land and the Village Green Foundation owns the building. We’re Friends of the Village Green — we look after the programs and the people who run them.

What the board would edit in Sanity

  • Every row under “What’s on” and every write-up under “What we’ve done” is one event document — the same one, before and after.
  • An event moves from the top list to the bottom list by itself, on its date. Nobody has to move it.
  • The write-up and photos are the bottom half of the event you already made. There is no second thing to remember.
  • “What we pay for” and the two donate blocks are a page, edited once or twice a year.
  • The program tags (VGAP, Greenworks, Village Green Woods) are a field on the event — not the navigation. Settling the pillars later changes nothing here.
