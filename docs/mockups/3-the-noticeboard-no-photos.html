Neighbours, keeping the Village Green busy.

Friends of the Village Green pays for and runs the programs at the Village Green Community Center in Kingston — concerts, a community garden, monthly lunches, lectures, and three acres of woods with trails through them. Free or low-cost, and open to every age and ability.

What we fund

More about what we do
  • Village Green Arts Program Sunday Sounds concerts, Music at the Green through the summer, and the exhibitions in the Grand Hallway Gallery.
  • Greenworks The community garden and its rental beds, plus classes, workshops and lectures. The 2026 beds sold out.
  • Village Green Woods Three acres above the upper parking lot. Volunteers build and clear trails on Wednesday mornings.
  • Lunches, lectures and welcomes The monthly senior luncheon with Kingston Cares, the newcomers series with the library, and four lectures a year.

Who’s who

Three groups share the name

The Metropolitan Park District owns the land. The Village Green Foundation owns the building. We are Friends of the Village Green, and we look after the programs and the people who run them.

What the board would edit in Sanity

  • Every card with a date on it is one event document. “What’s on” and the written-up cards are the same documents at two points in their life.
  • The coloured chips come from the program field on the event. When the board settles the pillars, the chips relabel themselves — the layout does not change.
  • “Next up” is not edited by hand. It is whichever event is soonest.
  • The quote is a field on a page, swapped when there’s a better one. It needs board permission before it goes live.
  • This layout tolerates thin weeks: with two events and one write-up it still looks full, because the cards reflow.
