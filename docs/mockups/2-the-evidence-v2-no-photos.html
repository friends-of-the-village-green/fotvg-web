Village Green Community Center · Kingston

What we help make happen.

Friends of the Village Green is an all-volunteer group. We support some of what goes on at the Village Green Community Center — the arts program, the Greenworks community garden, and community projects like the Secret Garden Tour. We don’t run or fund everything that happens here, and we hope to support more as Kingston grows.

Every Wednesday, July and August · Village Green Arts Program

Music at the Green

Free concerts in the Kingston Village Green Park on summer evenings, at half past six. Hot Club swing, jazz, Cajun, a string quartet, slide guitar blues — a different band every week.

People bring their own chairs and a good deal of food. By the second set there is usually dancing on the path.

Spring 2026 · Greenworks

New beds in the community garden

Volunteers laid out, built and filled a run of new cedar raised beds over a series of Saturday work parties — levelling the ground, cutting and screwing the frames, then barrowing in compost.

The beds are rented by the season to people in Kingston who have nowhere to grow. The 2026 beds sold out.

Autumn, each year · Greenworks

The orchard prune

A morning with ladders and loppers in the old apple trees. It is the sort of skill that is easier shown than written down, so the people who know bring the people who don’t.

Greenworks is the natural-world side of what we support: the garden, the orchard, and the classes and workshops that go with them.

Sunday, February 22, 2026 · Sunday Sounds

Cello Mania

Four cellists in the Summit room, playing folk, classical and contemporary pieces to a full house on a grey February afternoon.

Sunday Sounds runs through the winter, when the outdoor season is over and there is nowhere else to hear live music within twenty miles.

Changes through the year · Village Green Arts Program

The Grand Hallway Gallery

The corridor between the front desk and the hall doubles as a gallery, and the work on the walls changes through the year. Local painters, mostly.

It costs nothing to walk down it and nobody has to book, which is rather the point of hanging art in a corridor rather than a gallery.

Where the money goes

What we support

Three ongoing program areas at the moment. Plenty else goes on at the Village Green that we have no hand in — these are the ones we help pay for and put volunteers behind.

Village Green Arts Program

Arts and humanities. Sunday Sounds concerts, Music at the Green through the summer, and the rotating exhibitions in the Grand Hallway Gallery.

Greenworks

Community gardening, the natural sciences and the natural world. The garden and its rental beds, the orchard, and the classes and workshops that go with them. The 2026 beds sold out.

Community building

Community projects that raise money and bring people together — the NU2u rummage sale and the Secret Garden Tour.

And, we hope, more

We want to facilitate and support more activities and more community engagement, to meet the needs of a changing and growing Kingston.

Money

Donate

A donation pays a musician, buys seed and timber for a garden bed, or helps put on the rummage sale. We are an all-volunteer organization — no paid staff, no office, no one drawing a salary — so what you give goes to the program.

Volunteer

Wednesday mornings in the garden. An hour on the front table at Discover Kingston. Sorting donations for the rummage sale. Setting out chairs before a concert, and stacking them after. Tell us what you’d enjoy and we’ll find you something.
